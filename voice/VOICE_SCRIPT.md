# Voice Script: Batch 1 (counting prototype)

Thank you for being the voice of Dino Valley! These are the lines the counting
game says. Until your recordings arrive, the game uses the phone's robot voice.

## How to record (about 15 minutes)

1. Find a quiet room with soft things in it (a bedroom or closet is better
   than a kitchen). Turn off fans and the TV.
2. Use any voice recorder app on your phone. Hold the phone about a hand's
   width from your mouth, a little to the side so you don't puff into it.
3. Make **one long recording**. Before each line, say its number ("Number
   seven"), pause, then say the line. Leave about a second of silence after
   each line. If you stumble, just say the number again and redo it. Claude
   keeps the last take.
4. Smile while you talk. You're talking to a 4-year-old who is your biggest
   fan: warm, a little slower than normal, and excited at the happy parts.
5. Send the recording by attaching it in the project thread. Any format is
   fine (m4a, mp3, wav).

Tips: say the numbers as clear separate words ("one", "two"), not
sing-song. For the "Yes!" lines, celebrate as if he just did something amazing.

## Privacy note

These recordings ship inside the app. That's fine for family phones. If the
game is ever published on F-Droid, anyone who installs it would hear your
voice, so we'll ask again before then.
